## Article

**Website development cost in India: what changes the price**

If you ask five agencies what a website costs, you will get five numbers that are hard to compare. That is because the price follows the work, and the work depends on a handful of decisions you can make before you ask. This guide explains those decisions, and how we quote.

### The short answer

A business website in India is priced on four things: how many unique page layouts it needs, what it has to do beyond showing information, who supplies the content, and what happens after launch. ### What moves the price

**1. Page layouts, not page count.** Twenty service pages that share one layout cost far less than five pages that each need their own design. Count the layouts: home, a service page, a listing page, an article, contact.

**2. What the site has to do.** A site that only presents information is the simplest. Each of these adds design and build time:

- Booking or enquiry forms that carry context, such as the service a visitor was reading about
- Online payments and a cart
- Customer accounts and order history
- A content management system your team can use without a developer
- Integrations with a CRM, WhatsApp, email or accounting tools

**3. Content.** Writing, photography and product data are often the slowest part of a project. If you supply final text and images, the build is shorter. If we write and source them, that is added to the quote.

**4. Design depth.** Adapting an existing brand costs less than creating one. Custom illustrations and animation add time.

**5. After launch.** Hosting, a domain, security updates and small changes are ongoing costs. Ask every agency what is included in the first year.

### A real example of scope

Mr Antidot, a pest-control company in Ahmedabad, offers ten different treatments. Its site has one page per treatment, each with the estimated time, starting price, method and FAQs, and every "Book Now" button opens the contact form with that service already selected. That is one service-page layout used ten times, plus a booking form with a little logic. It shows how a site can be large in pages while staying modest in layouts.
